This release includes software from Ralomi (https://huggingface.co/ayousanz/ralomi,
Apache License 2.0, Copyright 2026 ayousanz): the log-Mel front-end
(code/koeact/features/logmel.py, ported from features.py and inference.py at revision
2f9f750b), the encoder (code/koeact/model/ralomi_torch.py, ported from StreamingDFSMNCTC
and DFSMNBlock in Ralomi's src/ralomi/model.py at commit 94ff2a94), and the CoreS3
firmware in cores3/firmware/e05 (Ralomi's esp32s3/firmware at revision 2f9f750b, with the
KoeAct decoder in cores3/firmware/a2 added). Building that firmware pulls ESP-DL, ESP-DSP,
M5Unified and M5GFX through the ESP-IDF component manager; their licenses are listed in
Ralomi's esp32s3/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. No firmware binary is included.

The weights in model.safetensors (KoeAct-JP, seed 14) and the CoreS3 artefacts in cores3/
(the same model: Ralomi quantized to W16A16 in 0.4 s chunks, and the decoder with int16
matrices) were built with the following materials. None of their audio or extracted
features is included in this release.

- Ralomi weights (https://huggingface.co/ayousanz/ralomi, revision 2f9f750b),
  Apache License 2.0, Copyright 2026 ayousanz: the starting point of the encoder.
  The release includes Ralomi's input layer and blocks 1-4 unchanged, and blocks 5-8 and
  the CTC classifier fine-tuned from Ralomi's weights.
- JapaneseTinyAgentLM-Action-Synth (revision 6ac72256), Apache License 2.0:
  training texts and Action labels.
- Amazon MASSIVE 1.1 (ja-JP), CC BY 4.0, Copyright Amazon.com Inc. or its affiliates
  (MASSIVE includes text derived from SLURP, CC BY 4.0): training texts.
- Irodori-TTS VoiceDesign (MIT): synthesized training speech from caption-designed
  voices (no real person's voice was cloned). Kokoro-82M (Apache License 2.0):
  synthesized validation speech.
- ReazonSpeech (Reazon Holdings), CDLA-Sharing-1.0, used under Article 30-4 of the
  Japanese Copyright Act: speech clips used as training negatives. ReazonSpeech audio,
  transcripts and features are not redistributed.
- OpenSLR SLR28 RIR and noise database, Apache License 2.0 (simulated RIRs and the
  point-source noises, which are the public-domain Freesound portion of MUSAN):
  augmentation and noise training negatives.

baseline/jtalm-kana-b1n/ is the comparison model: JapaneseTinyAgentLM's 3M text model
(code at commit e32b6c15, Apache License 2.0) retrained without JESC on the kana that
Ralomi read from the same synthesized training speech, with its SentencePiece tokenizer.
It is not a re-release of JapaneseTinyAgentLM-Action-3M.

Evaluation only (not used for training, and no audio from them is in this release):
MUSAN (Snyder et al.), CC BY 4.0; FSD50K (Fonseca et al., Freesound), CC BY 4.0, the CC0
and CC BY clips only.
